1. Who we are

CraftCutsDE Bot ("the app") is a private, non-commercial tool operated by the owner of the CraftCutsDE channels. Contact: CraftCutsDE@proton.me.

2. What data the app uses

The app is used only by the channel owner. When the owner connects their YouTube (Google) or TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic account information (account ID, display name, avatar) from that platform. The app uses this solely to upload videos to the owner's own account and to read the statistics of those videos.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. Google / YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. TikTok

The app uses the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit only to publish videos to the owner's account. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security > Manage app permissions).

6. Deletion

Stored tokens and data can be deleted at any time by revoking access or by emailing CraftCutsDE@proton.me.

Deutsch (Kurzfassung)

Der CraftCutsDE Bot wird nur vom Kanalinhaber genutzt. Er erhält von YouTube bzw. TikTok einen Zugriffsschlüssel und Basis-Kontodaten, um Videos auf das eigene Konto hochzuladen und deren Statistiken zu lesen. Alle Daten bleiben lokal auf dem Rechner des Inhabers, werden nicht weitergegeben und können jederzeit durch Entzug des Zugriffs oder per E-Mail gelöscht werden.
